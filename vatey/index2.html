Plant Care & Maintenance 📖

Watering Routine

Overwatering is the #1 plant issue! Always check if the top 2 inches of soil are dry before giving your plant a drink.

Sunlight Tips

Most indoor plants love bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid placing delicate leaves under direct harsh sunlight.

Humidity & Air

Tropical plants love humidity! Mist their leaves regularly or keep them near a humidifier for optimal growth.
